Solutions

High-stakes issues rarely present themselves as isolated challenges.

They sit at the intersection of policy, reputation, and stakeholder dynamics — and require a coordinated, disciplined response.

TDS Public Affairs works with organizations navigating complex public environments where outcomes are shaped not just by decisions, but by how they are understood, communicated, and supported.

Our work is grounded in strategy. From there, we help clients bring together the right combination of communications, stakeholder engagement, and public influence to move issues forward.

Engagement Model

How We Engage

We are typically engaged when clarity, credibility, and disciplined execution are most needed — and when the cost of reactive thinking is highest.

In these moments, our role is to help define the path forward and execute with precision.

We are typically engaged when:

  • An issue begins to attract public or political attention
  • Policy or regulatory decisions carry material consequences
  • Multiple stakeholders must be aligned around a common objective
  • Leadership requires clear strategy in a rapidly evolving environment

What We Offer

Core Areas of Work

01

Strategic Counsel

We advise leadership teams navigating complex public environments where policy, reputation, and stakeholder relationships intersect. Our work focuses on clarifying objectives, understanding the landscape, and defining the strategy that guides all subsequent action.

02

Narrative & Communications

Public issues are shaped by how they are understood. We help clients define clear, credible narratives and ensure those narratives are consistently reflected across communications, stakeholder engagement, and public discussion.

03

Stakeholder Engagement

Outcomes are rarely determined by a single decision maker. We help clients identify the stakeholders who influence outcomes and engage them in a way that builds credibility, alignment, and momentum.

04

Public Influence Campaigns

When issues require broader engagement, we design and execute coordinated efforts that bring together communications, advocacy, and coalition-building — structured to apply pressure at the right moments and in the right places.

05

Coalition Development

Credible coalitions can shape policy outcomes and strengthen public understanding. We help build and align coalitions that reflect real stakeholder interests and reinforce strategic objectives — designed not for breadth but for the specific decision being pursued.

How It Comes Together

Shaping Outcomes in Complex Environments

These capabilities are not deployed in isolation. Each engagement is built around a clear strategic objective, with communications, stakeholder engagement, and public influence working together to support that objective. This integrated approach ensures that effort is focused, consistent, and aligned with the outcome being pursued.

Clarity is often the first step toward shaping the right outcome.
